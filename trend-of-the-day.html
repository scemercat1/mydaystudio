Why Trend of the Day was discontinued

Posted: 12 November 2026

Trend of the Day was a small Chrome extension that showed the word of the day, a quote, a joke, a fun fact, the top Reddit post, YouTube trending videos and the weather. Over time, the services it relied on stopped responding, and keeping it running no longer made sense.

What happens now

The extension is no longer maintained. The features that still work have been moved into My Day, which is free and has no ads:

  • Word of the day, quote, fun fact and the top Reddit post in the Today tab
  • Weather for any city, with a what-to-wear tip
  • A focus timer, goals, a mood log and badges

Thank you

If you used Trend of the Day, thank you. If you'd like to follow the new projects, try My Day or contact us at junethecat07@gmail.com.
