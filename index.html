How to install

It takes about a minute.

  1. 1

    Click Add to Chrome, then Add extension.

  2. 2

    Click the puzzle icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin My Day.

  3. 3

    Click the My Day icon to open your day.

Accounts (optional)

You can use My Day without an account. To chat, create a profile in the Social tab. To keep your progress and use it on another computer, create an account in the Profile section with a username and password.
