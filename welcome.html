My Day

Thanks for installing My Day!

Your day, one click away. My Day is free and has no ads. Here's how to get started.

Get started

  1. 1

    Click Add to Chrome, then Add extension. You've already done this one.

  2. 2

    Click the puzzle icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin My Day.

  3. 3

    Click the My Day icon to open your day.
