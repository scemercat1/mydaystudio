Frequently asked questions

My Day doesn't load the daily research.

Some sources are free public services and can be slow or busy. Click Refresh on the Today tab. If one card still fails, it will try again the next time you open the app.

The chat doesn't show new messages.

The free room refreshes every few seconds while you're on the Social tab. Stay on the tab, or close and reopen the popup. Check your internet connection too.

How do I create a profile?

Open the Social tab, then Profile. Choose a username (private and permanent), a display name and an avatar, and click Create profile.

How do I save my progress and use it on another computer?

Create a profile first. In the Profile section, enter a password under Save account. On the other computer, open the Profile section and use Load account with the same username and password. Loading replaces the progress on that computer.

I forgot my password.

Email us from the address you used for your account, along with your username, and we'll help you recover it. Please don't include your password in the email.

How do I delete my data?

Email us with your username and ask us to delete your account. We'll remove your account, saved progress, profile and leaderboard entry.

The YouTube badge didn't unlock.

The OHYOUTUBE badge unlocks in the Chrome extension when you open youtube.com while the extension is installed. Reload the YouTube page once and check the Badges tab.

What is the mod code?

Moderators can remove messages from the free room. Mod access is earned in the app by following the steps in the Focus tab. Please keep the chat friendly.
