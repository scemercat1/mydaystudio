Privacy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

We don't sell your data or show ads.

What is stored on your computer

  • Your progress: streak, goals, focus sessions, mood log, reaction times, badges and weather cities you searched.
  • Your settings and the extension's login session, kept in Chrome's local extension storage.

What is stored online

Only if you use the online features:

  • The Social tab: your profile (username, display name, avatar and bio) and messages you send in the free room.
  • An account, if you create one: your username and a saved copy of your progress, so you can load it on another computer.

Website analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. It collects information such as the pages you view, how long you stay, your approximate location (country or region), your device and browser type, and the site that sent you here. It does not identify you personally, and it is not linked to your account in the extension.

When you first visit this website, you can accept or decline analytics. Analytics stays off until you accept. If you accept, Google Analytics sets cookies on this website. You can change your choice by clearing this site's data in your browser, or block the cookies in your browser settings. You can also install Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Google's privacy policy applies to the data it processes.

The extension itself does not send analytics data.

Who receives requests

The extension contacts the services that provide its content and features: Firebase (accounts, chat and saved progress), and public APIs for research, quotes, dictionary, Bible verses and weather. Those requests are sent directly from your browser. This website also sends analytics data to Google Analytics, as described above.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time in the Profile tab. Removing the extension deletes its local data. Online data is kept until it is deleted, so contact us if you want an account removed.
