Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy explains what information My Day collects, how it is used, and your choices. My Day is a daily planner made by It'sCHAOS. It is available as a Chrome extension and as a Windows app.

Contact

Questions, requests or deletion requests: junethecat07@gmail.com

What information My Day handles

  • Account details (optional): a username you choose, which is private and cannot be changed, and a password. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. My Day does not store your password in readable form.
  • Profile: a display name and an avatar. The display name and avatar are public. They appear in the free chat room and on the leaderboard. Your username is not shown to other users.
  • Chat messages: messages you send in the free room. They are visible to everyone who uses the free room.
  • Leaderboard score: a number calculated from your progress, shown next to your display name.
  • Progress: streaks, goals, mood check-ins, focus session counts, badges, game results and the weather cities you searched. This is stored on your device and, if you save an account, in your account's cloud copy.
  • Weather locations: the city names you search for in the weather tab.
  • Settings: your chosen language and, in the Windows app, an optional YouTube API key you enter yourself. These stay on your device.

What we do not collect

We do not sell your information, we do not show advertising, and we do not use advertising trackers. We do not collect your browsing history. The extension only counts how many research links you open; it does not store their addresses. The extension reads no page content, and it does not track what you do on other websites.

Services that receive information

  • Google Firebase stores accounts, chat messages, the leaderboard and saved progress.
  • Open-Meteo receives the city names you search for, to show weather and climate.
  • Public content services (Reddit, a Bible verse service, a quote service, a fun facts service and a dictionary service) receive a request when the daily research loads. The request includes your internet address, as with any website visit.
  • YouTube is only opened when you choose it. The extension detects that you opened youtube.com, to award a badge, and does not read the page.

How we use information

We use this information only to run the features you use: signing in, saving and loading your progress, showing chat messages and the leaderboard, showing weather, and keeping your progress across your devices. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to make credit or lending decisions.

Where information is stored

Local progress is stored on your computer or in your browser. Cloud data (accounts, chat messages, the leaderboard and saved progress) is stored by Google Firebase on servers that Google operates.

Moderation

A small number of users can be given moderator status. Moderators can delete chat messages in the free room. Please do not share personal information in the free room, because messages are visible to all users.

Children

My Day is not designed for children under 13. If you are under 13, use it only with the permission of a parent or guardian.

Your choices

  • Change your display name and avatar at any time in the Profile section.
  • Stop using My Day and remove the extension or app at any time.
  • Ask us to delete your account and cloud data by emailing junethecat07@gmail.com with your username. We will delete your account, saved progress, profile and leaderboard entry. We will also remove your chat messages on request.

Security

Passwords are protected by Firebase Authentication. Access to cloud data is limited by security rules: only you can read or change your saved progress, and only moderators can delete chat messages. No system is completely secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. For important changes, we will also tell users in the app.
